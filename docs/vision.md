# GameTracker Vision Document

Version: 1.0

## Product Name

GameTracker

## Product Category

Windows Desktop Application

Personal Gaming Analytics Platform

---

# Executive Summary

GameTracker is a Windows desktop application that automatically tracks gaming activity and transforms raw playtime into meaningful insights.

The application aims to provide gamers with complete visibility into their gaming habits across all launchers, platforms, and standalone games while maintaining a privacy-first and lightweight experience.

GameTracker is not intended to be another launcher or game manager.

Its purpose is to become the central source of truth for a player's gaming history and behavior.

---

# Problem Statement

Modern gamers often play games through:

* Steam
* Epic Games
* Riot Games
* Battle.net
* Ubisoft Connect
* Xbox Game Pass
* Standalone Games
* Emulators

As a result:

* Playtime data becomes fragmented.
* Historical gaming behavior is difficult to analyze.
* Long-term trends are hidden.
* Existing statistics are inconsistent or incomplete.

Most existing solutions answer:

"How long did I play?"

Very few answer:

"How do I spend my gaming time?"

---

# Vision Statement

To become the most trusted personal gaming analytics platform for Windows gamers.

GameTracker will help players understand, track, and improve their gaming habits through accurate playtime tracking, meaningful analytics, wellbeing-focused insights, and complete ownership of their gaming data.

---

# Mission Statement

To provide every gamer with accurate, transparent, and actionable insights about their gaming behavior regardless of where their games are installed or launched.

---

# Core Product Pillars

## Accuracy

Users must trust every statistic displayed.

Goals:

* Accurate session tracking
* Reliable process detection
* Crash recovery
* Shutdown recovery
* Session validation

---

## Insights

Data should answer meaningful questions.

Examples:

* Most played game
* Gaming trends
* Monthly changes
* Session behavior
* Activity patterns

---

## Wellbeing

The application should help users understand gaming habits rather than simply encourage more gaming.

Examples:

* Playtime goals
* Monthly targets
* Gaming balance reports
* Trend awareness

---

## Ownership

Users own their data.

Principles:

* Local-first storage
* Exportable history
* Backup support
* Optional cloud features

---

# Target Audience

Primary Audience:

* Windows PC Gamers
* Ages 16–40
* Multi-launcher users
* Statistics-oriented players
* Competitive and casual gamers

Secondary Audience:

* Streamers
* Content creators
* Gaming enthusiasts
* Digital wellbeing users

---

# Success Criteria

GameTracker is successful when:

* Users trust its statistics.
* Users keep it installed long-term.
* Users regularly review insights.
* Users recommend it to other gamers.
* It becomes part of their gaming routine.

---

# Long-Term Product Vision

GameTracker evolves from a playtime tracker into a personal gaming intelligence platform.

Future capabilities:

* Weekly reports
* Goal systems
* Cloud synchronization
* Multi-device analytics
* Gaming retrospectives
* Shareable statistics
* Advanced insights

The ultimate goal is to help gamers understand not only what they play, but how gaming fits into their lives.
